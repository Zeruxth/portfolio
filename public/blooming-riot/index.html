Roadmap

Made from unstable plant-based compounds, Polyflora mimics plastic but rejects permanence. It stiffens, bonds, and scents—only to collapse, unravel, and fade. What’s left is trace dust and the memory of form.

POLYFLORA IS A BIODEGRADABLE COMPOUND ENGINEERED FROM FLORA-DERIVED POLYMERS, DESIGNED TO DISAPPEAR, AND CREATED FOR THE FUTURE OF DAILY USE, CO-CREATED WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.

Prompt: “made of a semi-transparent green biomaterial with visible plant-vein structures, micro-cracks resembling dried chlorophyll, and a leaf-like organic texture that suggests slow decomposition”

OUR PRODUCTS

These products define the material’s visual, functional, and conceptual language. Each one demonstrates how Polyflora exists in context: minimal form, active surface, constant transformation. They serve as design anchors — a reference for texture, tone, and use. Future applications should maintain this balance between clarity and decay, control and change.

THE
DESIGNER

The Designer is a projected presence of Polyflora — built to handle, show, and behave. She appears selectively, in moments where the system needs a human proxy. Not as a user, but as an extension of the product itself. Her movements demonstrate the logic of the material: how it folds, resists, adapts. She does not speak. She performs. She is part of the interface. Use her as reference for interaction, proportion, and tone.

THE LOGO

the standalone symbol — represents the core duality of Polyflora: the meeting point between the organic and the synthetic. Built from modular geometry and interrupted by curved, irregular growths, it reflects the product’s nature — structured, but constantly shifting.

THE LOGOTYPE

the wordmark — expresses that same principle through typography. Clean, constructed letterforms are partially deformed: the broken “O”, the glitched “N”, the rigid framing. It’s language under tension, shaped by the material it names.

TOGETHER

Both elements are designed to function as part of the same system — but may operate independently when context requires.
